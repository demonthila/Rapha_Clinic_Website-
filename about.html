Our clinic

About Rapha Physio Care

Our focus is helping people recover from injuries, manage pain, restore mobility and improve their overall quality of life. We offer both clinic-based physiotherapy and home visits, making care accessible and convenient for patients who prefer treatment in the comfort of their own home.

We recognise that every patient has unique needs. That is why we provide evidence-based treatment, individualised rehabilitation programs and compassionate care, supporting each patient to reach their recovery goals and return to daily activities with confidence.

Meet your physiotherapist

Rincy Sam

Master of Physiotherapy (Orthopaedics)15+ years’ clinical experienceCertified in TMJ DisordersAHPRA registeredEnglish · Malayalam · Hindi

Rincy holds a Master’s degree in Orthopaedic Physiotherapy and has more than 15 years of clinical experience in musculoskeletal physiotherapy, across hospital and private practice. She is dedicated to helping people restore movement, relieve pain and improve their quality of life.

Her clinical experience includes the assessment and management of a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions, including back and neck pain, joint disorders, sports injuries, post-operative rehabilitation and movement-related problems.

Alongside her postgraduate qualification, Rincy holds an additional certification in Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ) Disorders, giving her focused training in the assessment and management of jaw-related pain and dysfunction.

Rincy takes a patient-centred approach, combining evidence-based treatment with individualised rehabilitation programs. Her goal is not only to relieve pain but to address its underlying causes, restore independence and help patients maintain long-term physical wellbeing.
