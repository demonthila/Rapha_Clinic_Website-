Privacy

Privacy Policy

Rapha Physio Care respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment time and a brief reason for your visit.

How we use it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry and arrange your appointment. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

Health information

Please do not send detailed medical information through the website form. Health information you provide during your care is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Access and questions

To access or correct your information, or if you have a question about privacy, contact us on 0450 119 053 or at connectraphaphysio@gmail.com.
