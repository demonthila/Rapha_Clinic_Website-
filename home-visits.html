Home visits

Physiotherapy in the comfort of your own home

If getting to a clinic is difficult, Rincy can come to you. Home visits are ideal after surgery, for older adults, for people with limited mobility, or simply when home is more convenient.

Areas we visit

Truganina and nearby suburbs

TruganinaTarneitWerribeePoint Cook

Living just outside these areas? Get in touch and we’ll let you know if we can help. Please contact us for home visit fees.
